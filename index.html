INDEPENDENT PROJECT / AUG 2026–PRESENT

A quantitative research
platform, built from the data up. ↗

A Python-based workspace bringing market data, interpretable signals and strategy validation together across A-shares, ETFs and options.

9registered programs
7cloud modules
18research signals
  • Developed a Python-based quantitative research platform, registered nine programs and deployed seven cloud modules, integrating QMT, Wind and public-market APIs across A-shares, ETFs and options.
  • Validated 24,241 records across nine datasets and screened 5,548 A-share securities at a 98.9% retrieval success rate, with source, date, missing-value and non-synthetic-data checks.
  • Built two research engines with 18 signals, including six A-share sentiment factors and 12 Hong Kong equity alpha factors, applying weighted scoring and point-in-time as-of controls.
  • Backtested a moving-average and volatility strategy using isolated training and holdout sets, selecting parameters by excess Sharpe ratio less a 0.25 turnover penalty and incorporating commissions, stamp duty and slippage.
